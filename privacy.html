Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Blackjack Sense mobile app ("App"), operated by Arvin Gonzales ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information about you. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account identifiers. When you first open the App, we create an anonymous user ID via Firebase Authentication. If you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive a stable Apple-provided user identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email address (or Apple's private relay address).

Profile data you provide. Optional username and emoji avatar you choose in the App.

In-app state. Your progress through lessons, drill attempt history, in-app currency balances (bankroll, loan bonus, debt), and settings. These are stored locally and synced to Firebase Firestore linked to your account ID so your progress persists across devices.

Subscription state. Whether you have an active Pro subscription. Subscription transactions are processed by Apple; we do not receive your payment method or full purchase history — only the entitlement status via Apple's StoreKit APIs.

Device and diagnostic data. Basic device information (operating system version, device model, app version, coarse locale) collected by Firebase for stability and abuse-prevention purposes. Firebase App Check may generate a device-attestation token to prevent API abuse.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App;
  • To sync your progress and settings across your devices;
  • To validate purchases and unlock content you have paid for;
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or violations of our Terms;
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services to operate the App:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, StoreKit. Handles authentication (if you choose) and processes all subscription payments.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, App Check). Stores your profile data and in-app state, and helps us guard against tampering with server calls.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Firebase's Privacy Information.

4. Advertising

Blackjack Sense does not currently show third-party advertisements and does not sell your personal data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (see below), we will delete or anonymize your profile data within 30 days, except where longer retention is required by law or for fraud prevention.

6. Your Choices

Access, correction, deletion. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, email arvingonz@gmail.com from the address associated with your Apple ID. We'll respond within 30 days.

Managing your subscription. You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in your device's App Store settings, under your Apple ID.

7. Children

Blackjack Sense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Users

The App is operated from the United States and data is processed on servers located in the United States and other regions where our third-party providers operate. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your data to these locations.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures (Firebase App Check, Firestore security rules) to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email arvingonz@gmail.com.
